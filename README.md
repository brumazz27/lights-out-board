# Lights Out Board

A community board for reporting cars seen with burnt-out headlights, tail lights, brake lights, turn signals, low or flat tires, and other easy-to-miss problems.

- `lights-out.html` — the page. People post a sighting (plate optional, color, make and model, problem, location, time, notes) and see everyone's reports in a live list they can filter by problem and by open or fixed.

The page is built as a Claude artifact and keeps its reports in the artifact's shared database, so saving reports only works when it's opened from its claude.ai artifact link.
